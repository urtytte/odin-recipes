Pizza sauce

This is a recipe for a great pizza sauce!

It is inspired by the italian classic flavous. The products used in the recipe should ideally come from an italian produce store!

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup water, or as needed
  • 1 (6 ounce) can tomato paste
  • ⅓ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ tablespoon dried oregano
  • ½ tablespoon dried basil
  • ½ teaspoon dried rosemary, crushed
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Mix together water, tomato paste, and olive oil in a large bowl or jar. Add garlic, oregano, basil, rosemary, salt, and pepper; mix well.
  3. For best results, let sauce stand for several hours to allow the flavors to blend.
  4. No cooking is necessary; just spread on pizza crust.

Now it's time to enjoy!
